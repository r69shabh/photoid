BEFORE YOU SUBMIT

Good to know.

Where does my photo go?

Nowhere. Your browser handles the photo, camera, crop, background and exports. Automatic framing and background removal download models, not your image. Gumroad only receives the key when you activate Pro.

Will my application accept the photo?

PhotoID does not certify eligibility. Check the official requirements for your specific application, including head size, background and whether edits are allowed. Use your original background where edited photos are prohibited. Do not submit a watermarked preview.

How do I print at the right size?

Pick the paper size in PhotoID and in your print dialog, then choose Actual size or 100%. Turn off “Fit to page.” Measure one cut rectangle before printing further copies.

How do I unlock Pro?
